Our Story

beautyBUT started with a simple frustration: why is it so hard to find beauty products that are both effective and honest?

Too many products hide behind fancy packaging and complicated ingredient lists. We wanted something different — formulas you can trust, ingredients you can pronounce, and a brand that doesn't test on animals. Ever.

Every product we make is designed for real skin, real routines, and real people. Because beauty shouldn't be complicated — it should just work.
